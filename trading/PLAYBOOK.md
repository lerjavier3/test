# Playbook: strategies that have proven themselves

For later use with real money. A strategy is listed here only when all of these hold:

1. Backtest on at least 1 year of data is profitable after costs in sample AND out of sample (the last 120 days, never used for tuning): profit factor above 1 in both.
2. Live paper results after at least 15 to 20 trades agree with the backtest.
3. The numbers are measured unlevered, per trade, so "works without leverage" means the profit factor and average win vs average loss hold at 1x. Leverage only scales a positive edge; it cannot create one.

Each entry gives the exact rules, indicators, win rate, average win vs average loss, profit factor and whether it works without leverage. Numbers come from `trading/journal/strategy-scoreboard.md` (backtests on Alpaca data, live from Alpaca fills).
